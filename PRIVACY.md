# Privacy Policy for BankOfFavors (بنك الحسنات)

**Effective date:** 30 September 2026

BankOfFavors ("the App") is developed by KGSOFT. Your privacy matters to us.
This policy explains what the App does and does not do with your information.

## Information the App does NOT collect

- The App does not require an account and does not ask for your name, email, or phone number.
- The App contains no advertisements and no advertising trackers.
- The App contains no analytics or usage-tracking SDKs.
- We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with anyone.

## Location (used only for prayer times)

- If you grant location permission, the App uses your approximate coordinates
  solely to fetch accurate prayer times for your area.
- The coordinates are sent to the Aladhan prayer-times API (api.aladhan.com),
  and any city name you type is sent to the Open-Meteo geocoding API
  (open-meteo.com), only to return prayer timings. These services receive only
  what is needed to answer the request.
- Your location is stored on your device only, so the App does not ask you
  every time. You can deny the permission and enter a city manually instead.

## Data stored on your device

- The App stores your preferences locally on your device (language, saved
  location, tasbih counters, settings). This data never leaves your device
  except for the prayer-times requests described above.

## Notifications

- The App may send local notifications (e.g., prayer reminders). These are
  generated on your device; no data is sent to us.

## Children

- The App does not knowingly collect information from children.

## Changes to this policy

- If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address.

## Contact

- If you have questions about this policy, contact us via the repository:
  https://github.com/muzammilsoft/BankOfFavors/issues
